How PawRoute Works

From finding your match to caring for their coat for years to come — here is our straightforward process.

1
🔎

Discover & Match

Browse verified pet profiles, filter by lifestyle and home environment, and save your favourite candidates with one click.

2
📋

Submit Inquiry

Fill out a simple matching questionnaire. In production, our shelter coordinators review your home setup within 48 hours.

3
🤝

Meet & Greet

Schedule a calm 1-on-1 visit at our Tallinn partner facility to spend time together and ensure a perfect personality bond.

4
🏡

Welcome Home & Care

Finalize adoption with an EU passport & microchip transfer, then book fear-free grooming checkups to keep them happy!
