About PawRoute & Adoption Guidelines

We connect vetted shelter animals with loving Nordic homes, and provide gentle grooming spa treatments so every companion thrives.

Our Care Mission

A Lifelong Commitment to Every Pet

Founded in Tallinn, PawRoute was created to address a critical need: uniting ethical pet adoption with post-adoption coat and skin care. We believe adoption is just day one of a beautiful journey.

💚

Fear-Free Certified

We never rush. Whether acclimatizing a rescue pet or grooming a sensitive senior, patience comes first.

🧴

Organic & Pure Shampoos

Only paraben-free, hypoallergenic botanical balms designed for harsh northern winter and summer coat changes.

🤝

Ethical Rescue Network

Partnered with trusted Estonian shelters across Harju County to ensure transparent medical records and loving placements.

💶

Transparent Estonian Rates

No hidden fees. Every service package and adoption contribution is clearly stated in Euros (€).

Step-by-Step Rehoming

The PawRoute Adoption Process

Our four simple steps to ensure a harmonious match between pet temperament and household environment.

1
🔎

1. Search & Favourite

Explore verified rescue profiles on our Find Pets page. Tap the ❤️ heart to build your shortlist.

2
📋

2. Submit Questionnaire

Complete a simple lifestyle questionnaire so coordinators can evaluate household fencing, other pets, and work schedules.

3
🤝

3. 1-on-1 Meet & Greet

Visit our Tallinn partner foster location for a relaxed walk and cuddle session to ensure a genuine personality connection.

4
🏡

4. Welcome Home

Sign the adoption agreement, receive the official EU Pet Passport & microchip registry transfer, and enjoy your welcome grooming bath!
